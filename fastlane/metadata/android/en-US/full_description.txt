QuickExpense is built around one idea: saving a purchase should take seconds. Enter the amount, pick where the money came from, tap "Today" — done. Sorting it into categories, splitting the receipt and tagging can wait until you have a minute.

<b>Fast entry</b>
• Big number pad, one-tap "Today" / "Yesterday", calendar for anything older
• Home-screen widget that opens straight into a new expense and shows how much you've spent today, this week or this month — or how much is left of your limit
• Voice input: say the amount and the shop or category, e.g. "250 coffee Lidl", and the form fills itself in from your own lists
• Favourite sources, categories and recipients always one tap away

<b>Receipts</b>
• Scan long receipts page by page — an overlay of the previous page helps you line up the next one
• Receipts are attached to the expense and easy to find later, e.g. for a warranty claim
• Save any receipt photo to your gallery when you need it outside the app

<b>Splits and categories</b>
• Break one purchase into parts: groceries, household, a gift — each with its own category
• Split parts further into sub-items; the unallocated rest is always shown
• Tags for anything that cuts across categories: a trip, a renovation, "for work"

<b>Statistics</b>
• Dashboard with totals, spending by category, recipient and tag, and a daily trend
• Comparison with the previous period
• Detailed search by text, category, recipient and tags
• Export to CSV (ZIP) and share it anywhere

<b>Private by design</b>
QuickExpense has no internet access at all. No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Available in English, Russian and Serbian.
